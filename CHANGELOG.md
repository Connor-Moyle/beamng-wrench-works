# Changelog: BeamNG Wrench Works (garage)

## Unreleased (release 1 candidate; proposed version 1.7.0, now 1.6.0)

What is new since 1.6.0, for players:

- **Shop opening hours in a Street Legal career.** The parts shop counters close outside 08:00-18:00 and tell you when they open ("Closed. Opens at 08:00"). Outside a career the shops stay open.
- **Garage doors, much calmer.** Entering the garage by stopping on its marker works whichever way the car faces, and on foot in a career the doorway takes you in with your current car. Doors that would not shut after a visit are fixed: a kept dent no longer breaks a latch for good, and every door and lid is latched when the car comes out.
- **Garage on a gamepad.** The right stick orbits the camera, the zoom buttons zoom, the centre-camera button resets the view and B closes a panel. Tooltips also show on focus.
- **Car radio while you drive.** The garage radio's music plays in the car too: a station per music folder (loose tracks are "Wrench Works FM"), keys for on/off and next station, and each car remembers its station.
- **Parts catalog and dyno.** A new "Under $1,500" filter for cheap upgrades (it shows when it is on). The dyno draws your first (or pinned) run dashed under each new run and shows the gain at the peaks.
- **More settings.** A Driver group (who sits in the car, shirt, trousers, size, helmet, and how it moves), an Injuries setting and a World group for the pedestrians, shown only when Drivers & Pedestrians is installed.
- **Job cars.** A part taken off a customer's car sells for nothing and can only go back onto a job car, not onto one of yours.
- **For other mods' careers.** What the garage has (lift, workbench, dyno, paint booth, photo corner, shelf size) can be set by another mod, and the shelf, repairs, dyno sheet, part sales and drive-out are reported to it.

Fixed: cancelling a garage visit no longer pulls you back into the garage car.
